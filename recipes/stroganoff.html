Beef Stroganoff, also spelled beef Stroganov, is a Russian dish of sautéed pieces of beef in a sauce of mustard and smetana (heavy sour cream). It is named after one of the members of the Stroganov family. Since its appearance in the 19th century, it has become popular around the world, with considerable variation from the original recipe. -Wikipedia


List of Ingredients:

  • Thinly sliced steak
  • Mushrooms
  • Garlic
  • Sour Cream
  • Worcestershire Sauce

Steps to make:

  1. Start by searing thin strips of beef in butter or oil until browned
  2. Remove them from the skillet.
  3. Sauté sliced mushrooms in the same pan until softened
  4. Add garlic
  5. Stir in flour to create a roux
  6. Add beef broth and Worchestershire sauce
  7. Remove the pan from heat
  8. Stir in sour cream
  9. Serve! :)
