Hamburger

Home

A hamburger is a food consisting of one or more cooked patties—usually ground meat, typically beef—placed inside a sliced bun or bread roll. Hamburgers are often served with cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, and condiments like ketchup, mustard, or mayonnaise.


List of Ingredients:

  • Ground beef (80/20 blend recommended)
  • Hamburger buns
  • Cheddar or American cheese slices
  • Lettuce and sliced tomato
  • Sliced onions and pickles
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Condiments (Ketchup, Mustard, Mayonnaise)

Steps to make:

  1. Divide ground beef into portions and gently form them into patties.
  2. Season both sides generously with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat a skillet or grill over high heat.
  4. Cook patties for 3 to 4 minutes on the first side until a nice crust forms.
  5. Flip patties, add a slice of cheese on top, and cook for another 2 to 3 minutes.
  6. Lightly toast the hamburger buns on the grill or skillet.
  7. Assemble by placing the patty on the bottom bun, adding toppings and sauces, and covering with the top bun.
  8. Serve hot! :)
