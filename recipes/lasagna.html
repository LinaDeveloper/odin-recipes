Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made of stacked layers of flat pasta alternated with fillings such as ragù (ground meats and tomato sauce), vegetables, cheeses, and seasonings. Originating in Italy during the Middle Ages, it is now a widely popular comfort food across the globe.


List of Ingredients:

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef or Italian sausage
  • Marinara sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Grated Parmesan cheese
  • Egg, garlic, and herbs

Steps to make:

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package directions and drain.
  2. Brown the ground meat in a pan, add garlic, and stir in marinara sauce.
  3. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese with an egg and Italian herbs.
  4. In a baking dish, spread a thin layer of sauce, then layer noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella.
  5. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, ending with a top layer of mozzarella and Parmesan.
  6. Bake in a preheated oven at 375°F (190°C) for about 45 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  7. Let it cool for 10 minutes before slicing and serving! :)
